#### *5. OUR SERVICES*
We offer complete turnkey and specialized construction solutions:

1. *Project Management*  
   Full oversight from planning to handover. Budget control, scheduling, contractor coordination, and quality assurance to ensure smooth delivery.

2. *Plans - Architectural & Structural Drafting*  
   Professional building plans, permit drawings, and 3D designs compliant with City of Bulawayo and national building codes.

3. *Building - Residential & Commercial*  
   New builds, extensions, renovations, and institutional structures. Brickwork, plastering, tiling, and finishes to the highest standard.

4. *Roofing*  
   Supply and installation of IBR, Q-Tile, and flat roofing. Roof repairs, waterproofing, gutters, and fascia for durability in all weather.

5. *Site Assessment*  
   Soil testing, topography survey, access evaluation, and risk assessment to determine the best approach for your project.

6. *Project Suitability Assessment*  
   Feasibility studies to assess if your project is viable. We evaluate budget, zoning, services, and timelines before you commit.

7. *Plumbing*  
   Complete plumbing systems: water reticulation, sewer, drainage, geysers, and bathroom/kitchen fittings. Leak detection and maintenance.

8. *Electrification*  
   Domestic and commercial electrical installations. Wiring, DB boards, lighting, solar integration, and ZESA compliance certification.
